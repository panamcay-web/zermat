## Who is responsible

{operator}, {address}. Email: {email}.

## What we collect

- Your booking: arrival and departure dates, licence plate, parking option and any Zermatt Shuttle tickets.
- Your email address, to send your confirmation and messages about your subscription.
- Your order number and the time you gave your consent at checkout.
- If you send a cancellation request: your email address and, if you give it, your order number.
- Usage data from our website statistics (see below).

## Why we use it

To make and manage your booking and subscription, to bill you, to answer your requests and to meet our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you and our legal obligations. For website statistics, it is our legitimate interest or, where required, your consent.

## Who receives it

- Our payment provider, to process payments.
- FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers booking and cancellation forms to our mailbox.
- Yandex Metrica, our website statistics service. It uses cookies and can record clicks and scrolling on our pages.
- Our website hosting provider.

We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it

As long as your subscription is active, and after that for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

## Cookies and browser storage

Our website uses cookies for statistics (Yandex Metrica). It also keeps your booking draft and your language choice in your browser, so you don't lose them when you reload the page.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. Just write to {email}. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
