Arriving by car

How to drive to the Matterhorn Terminal in Täsch and where to leave the car for Zermatt.

Zermatt is car-free, so every road trip to the Matterhorn ends in Täsch.

Parking at the terminal

The terminal is a covered multi-storey car park with around 2,100 spaces.

In short: book online before you set off. Your plate is recognised at the barrier.

The height limit

Maximum height for vehicles is 2.30 metres.

From the car to Zermatt

The shuttle-train platform is a two-minute walk from the garage.
