Reduced mobility & accessibility

Accessible parking at the terminal and step-free access to the shuttle train.

The Matterhorn Terminal is designed to be step-free.

Parking close to the lifts

Reserve a standard space online in the usual way.

Onto the train and into Zermatt

Lifts and ramps connect the car park to the platform. Bring what you need for a smooth transfer:

  • Your parking confirmation (tied to your licence plate)
  • A valid disabled parking badge for the accessible bays
