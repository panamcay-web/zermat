Parking info

Everything you need to know about parking at the Matterhorn Terminal in Täsch and reaching car-free Zermatt.

Good to know: Zermatt is car-free. Every car stops in Täsch: park at the Matterhorn Terminal and continue by shuttle train or taxi.
