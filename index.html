Park & ride to Zermatt

Zermatt is car-free. Leave your car at the Matterhorn Terminal in Täsch and take the shuttle train — it runs about every 20 minutes and reaches Zermatt in around 12 minutes.

Book online in a few clicks. After payment, you'll receive a confirmation email. Your licence plate is registered at the barrier, so number-plate recognition lets you drive straight into the covered garage on arrival — no ticket to lose.

The terminal has around 2,100 covered spaces and 131 e-charging stations. Choose “Parking with e-charging station” when you book to reserve a charging bay for your electric car.

Advantages

Everything you get when you book with us

  • Covered garage

    A weather-protected space in the multi-storey terminal, whatever the mountain weather does.

  • Plate recognition

    Your licence plate opens the barrier automatically — drive straight in, no ticket needed.

  • Shuttle to Zermatt

    The train to car-free Zermatt leaves right from the terminal, roughly every 20 minutes — add your tickets straight to your parking booking.

  • E-charging on site

    131 charging stations for electric cars — reserve a space with a charger when you book.
